# The goal's questions, listed from docs/GOAL.md by list_questions.py

- `docs/GOAL.md:150` 1. **A date that is not a real date.** A date of birth in the form YYYY-MM-DD that names no day of the calendar, such as 2019-02-30: is it refused, and on which screen, or is it searched for and saved?
- `docs/GOAL.md:151` 2. **An age outside 6 to 12.** The ages 6 to 12 are those of the made-up data: when the clerk enters a date of birth that makes the child younger than 6 or older than 12 on the date ages are reckoned on, or that falls after that date, is the entry refused, or is the child enrolled?
- `docs/GOAL.md:152` 3. **A learner number that cannot be given.** When the registry cannot give a learner number or cannot save the record: what does the clerk see, and what is saved?
- `docs/GOAL.md:153` 4. **What a name may hold.** Is there a longest family name or given names, are any characters refused, and is an entry of spaces only an entry made or an entry missing?
- `docs/GOAL.md:154` 5. **An entry missing (F-1).** The sources say only that the search runs after the clerk has entered all four (`groundwork/search_rule.csv:10`; the ruling on G-07: "all four entered before the search runs"). Can the clerk submit with one of the four not entered; if so, what does the clerk see — the enrolment screen as it was, a message, or a refusal of the entry — and on which screen?
- `docs/GOAL.md:155` 6. **A school not among the three (F-3).** The school is "chosen from the data's three" (the ruling on G-07; `groundwork/settings.csv:18`): can a school not among them reach the registry at all? If it can, what does the clerk see, is the entry refused, and is anything saved?
- `docs/GOAL.md:156` 7. **The order of the date check and the search.** Two sets of passages do not settle which comes first. On one side, the ruling on G-07, `docs/GAPS-PAPER.md:254`: "the date entered as YYYY-MM-DD and refused otherwise, the school chosen from the data's three, and all four entered before the search runs", with `groundwork/settings.csv:17`: "YYYY-MM-DD; any other form refused". On the other, the brief's line 11: "The registry searches for the child first", R-04: "the registry searches for the child before it does anything else with the entry", and `groundwork/search_rule.csv:11`: "first: before the registry does anything else with the entry". Is a date in the wrong form refused before the search runs, or does the search run first; and so, for such a date, does the search run, and can a record be saved before the refusal?
- `docs/GOAL.md:157` 8. **What a refusal says.** What do SC-4 and SC-5 say beyond that the date of birth is refused or that the entry cannot be completed, and are the clerk's four entries kept on the enrolment screen afterwards?
- `docs/GOAL.md:158` 9. **More than one fault at once.** When more than one of F-1, F-2 and F-3 holds at once: does the clerk see every fault, or one only, and which?
- `docs/GOAL.md:159` 10. **What SC-2 shows of the learner found.** Besides its learner number, which of the learner's fields does the screen show?
- `docs/GOAL.md:160` 11. **A different school on a learner found.** When exactly one learner is found and the school the clerk chose is not the learner's school: is the clerk told, and is anything changed?
- `docs/GOAL.md:161` 12. **A different child sharing the three fields.** A child the registry does not hold whose family name, given names and date of birth, capitals ignored, are those of a learner it holds — the school not compared — is found as that learner and nothing new is entered: is this a way the goal goes wrong, and what is the clerk to do?
- `docs/GOAL.md:162` 13. **After the result.** Once the clerk has seen SC-2, SC-3, SC-4 or SC-5, how does the clerk begin the next enrolment: does the prototype return to SC-1?
